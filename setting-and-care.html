Setting and caring for a calendar watch

Step-by-step instructions to set the day, date and moon phase safely, and simple habits that keep the mechanism healthy.

StepsSet

Setting a day-date watch

  1. Move to safety

    Pull the crown to the time-setting position and move the hands to about 6:30.

  2. Set the date

    Use the quick-set position to reach the day before today’s date.

  3. Set the day

    On many watches, turning the crown the other way changes the day. Set it to yesterday.

  4. Advance the time

    Move the hands forward past midnight until the day and date flip, then set the correct time, checking am or pm.

CareHabits

Habits that protect the calendar

HabitWhy it helps
Avoid the 9 pm–3 am window when using the quick-setPrevents strain on engaged date gears
Wind or wear automatic watches regularlyKeeps the calendar running so it needs less resetting
Use a watch winder for perpetual calendars if suitableSaves complex resetting after the watch stops
Push or screw the crown back in after settingMaintains water resistance
Service as the maker recommendsFresh lubrication keeps calendar wheels moving smoothly

Do

  • Check the manual for your movement’s safe zone.
  • Use the correction pusher tool supplied with recessed pushers.
  • Note the next month-end correction in your calendar.

Don’t

  • Force the crown if it feels stiff.
  • Move the hands backwards through midnight on complex calendars.
  • Use a pin or pen to press recessed correctors.
